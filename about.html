We began operations in Malaysia in late 1997, and have since integrated general practice (GP) clinics, dental clinics, medical imaging centres, and therapy and rehabilitation centres across Malaysia into our networks as well as managing an ambulatory care centre. Expansion is continuing in Malaysia. Our significant presence in urban and suburban markets in Malaysia coupled with unique synergy of corporate expertise, multidisciplinary medicine and strong local relationships has made Qualitas Health Malaysia one of the reliable and fastest growing healthcare companies.

With more than 20 years of experience in the healthcare industry to date, Qualitas Health is well established in Malaysia, Singapore and Australia, with a growing presence in the Asia Pacific region.
